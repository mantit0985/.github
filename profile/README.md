# Manny Titsidu

Systems engineer. I build and maintain systems until they do what they are supposed to do — no more, no less.

I design by living through a build, not by pre-writing it: **life is lived by the second and learned by the hour.** Anything outside that scope is either history or a future born in the next.

## How I work

- **Verify before "done."** Nothing counts until the evidence lands — a claim I can't reproduce is a claim I don't keep.
- **Reversibility is a feature.** Spend caps, flip-back paths, no half-measures shipped.
- **Delegate deliberately.** One owner per workstream, no idle hands, an audit trail for every decision.
