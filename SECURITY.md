# Security Policy

## Vulnerability Disclosure

The security of our projects is a priority. We encourage the responsible disclosure of any discovered vulnerabilities.

## Reporting Process

To report a security issue:

1. **Confidentiality**: Do not open a public issue.
2. **Submission**: Use the GitHub **Report a vulnerability** feature or contact the maintainer directly.
3. **Details**: Include a detailed description of the vulnerability, step-by-step reproduction instructions, and the potential impact.

## Our Commitment

- **Acknowledgement**: We will acknowledge receipt of the report within 48 hours.
- **Resolution**: We aim to provide a fix or mitigation as efficiently as possible.
- **Disclosure**: We follow coordinated disclosure. Vulnerabilities will not be disclosed publicly until a patch is available or a reasonable period has elapsed.
